UniTools World Recipes v2.0.0, by UniTools — theunitools.com
Source: https://theunitools.com/data/unitools-recipes-v1.json
License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The distributed subset is an adaptation by MassTracker under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Changes include selection, categorization, conversion of ingredient quantities
to reference masses, recalculation of nutrition, and omission of photos.
The optional Recipe Expansion pack carries 459 UniTools recipes. MassTracker
links verified ingredient matches and keeps source quantities for ingredients
without verified nutrition or weight. These recipes have incomplete nutrition.
